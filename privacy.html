Privacy policy

Who uses this app

Agent Workspace is a personal tool. It is used only by its owner, Kostas, on his own computer, and is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

With his permission, given on Google's own consent screen, the app reads and, only when he asks, changes his own Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar through Google's APIs. It requests nothing else.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is fetched only to answer his own requests, and is processed on his computer.
  • The sign-in is stored encrypted on his computer. No copy of his Google data is kept on any server run for this app.
  • When he asks an AI assistant he uses (such as Anthropic's Claude or Google's Gemini) about his mail, files or calendar, the parts needed for that answer are sent to that assistant under its own terms.
  • His data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Agent Workspace's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
